# Incumbent with surgical enforcement and evidence repairs

Retain the incumbent's semantic model and repair its enforcement and evidence account. The new experiments support the range importer, canonical PR continuity, separate authored archives, bounded after-ack recovery and native ownership. They do not establish that this is a complete replacement: the provider handoff for overlapping suffix writes remains unresolved, the local recovery prototype violates required invariants, and author/reviewer usability remains untested. No change is therefore a useful comparison baseline, not an acceptance verdict.

This is Architect γ's bounded proposal under the fixed C1–C8 contract. Root owns comparison, synthesis, confirmation and stopping. I read the frame, contract, corrected incumbent, all five follow-up reports, local/structural case matrices and the final evidence audit. The concrete cases below are exposed design witnesses. I did not read sealed cases, old sealed material, other architect proposals or coordinator advocacy. Private agent context and a partitioned write directory provide task isolation; the shared filesystem has no OS ACL isolation. Requested model/effort is inherited; the exact effective configuration is unknown. No experiment, production edit or external action was performed for this proposal.

## What should survive

The incumbent's distinctions have counterexamples that a smaller vocabulary would conceal.

1. **Authored submission, incorporated hosted range and accepted local result differ.** Archive A at `x=0`; import hosted `x=1` while retaining unpublished local `y=1`; then import hosted `x=2`. The next merge must use incorporated `x=1`, preserving `y=1`. Reusing the authored baseline can manufacture a conflict; using the accepted local result as hosted baseline can consume pending local content. L03 observes these negative controls. L02 adds eighteen changing-parent A/B rounds with independently expected trees, ownership and messages. These roles may share one record; collapsing their meaning is unsound. Scratch commit regeneration on unchanged import, L04, also does not authorize a new authored archive or remote write. [Local results and matrix](../../local/RESULTS.md), [L01–L30](../../local/CASE-MATRIX.md).

2. **An admission result, queue membership, PR lifecycle and usable suffix revision differ.** In the queue cases, terminal `enqueued` persists after landing or removal; an empty queue can follow partial landing. SQUASH/build-one lands A and ejects rewritten B, while tested build-two configurations land both. A scalar success or a method-only support flag loses facts needed for import and a distinct later admission. Recording these observations separately costs more than a Boolean, but avoids inventing success for B. [Queue results](../../queue/RESULTS.md).

3. **Canonical review identity is independent of native grouping.** The nine structural after-ack recoveries preserve all original PR identities and exact archives while completing new grouping. Conversely, historical or locked members can remain in residual groups. Stable PR bindings plus replaceable native group identity describe this without a second maintained dependency graph. Registration alone cannot establish ownership, archive correctness or incorporation. [Structural results](../../structure/RESULTS.md).

4. **Generated history and human-owned PR prose need separate ownership.** The distinct-actor experiment updates a bot comment and the user-account body with actual interleaving while preserving both payloads. A stale whole-body writer has a demonstrated overwrite hazard. A separate artifact does not solve discovery or authorization by itself: the bot could also edit a user-owned comment, and a foreign actor copied the exact marker. Keep explicit artifact binding and conflict handling; do not call author identity a provider-enforced exclusive writer. [CI/comment results](../../ci-reviews/RESULTS.md).

5. **Captured input and live authoring state differ.** The fresh-continuation policy preserves a moving source ref, dirty/staged/untracked bytes and its old checkpoint, at the cost of another branch selection. L10 and L24 support that boundary. An in-place reset or a hook inferred from outer push intent reintroduces concrete loss or surprise: outer dry-run, unrelated-ref and deletion pushes each triggered the fixture hook's independent publication, L23. No human convenience result favors either invocation yet. [Local results](../../local/RESULTS.md).

These arguments defend meanings, not separate databases, state-machine frameworks or commands. Preparation may occur inside submission, and one evaluator can serve preview, status and dispatch.

## Surgical candidate text

The coordinator can preserve the incumbent and make the following bounded clarifications. These are proposed requirements and acceptance criteria, not implemented repairs.

**Enforce admission where effects occur.** Every mutation/recovery entry point must use the admitted operation evidence and cooperating-owner mechanism for the affected chain, including linked worktrees. Before dispatch, obtain that local ownership and reread the durable state and bindings; do not act on a previously cached `planned` snapshot. A pending manifest must resolve to the same operation and captured intent. Missing or inconsistent evidence enters recovery rather than reconstructing a new operation. Completion recovery must validate the claimed durable continuation/checkpoint and exact accepted result, or an explicitly recorded successor/handoff. Transfer must preserve those facts and unresolved effects.

This validation must preserve normal authoring: a completed continuation can later receive accepted amendments or rebases. The immutable accepted result and incorporated checkpoint remain distinct from the current mutable source range. Requiring the live branch to equal its original continuation SHA forever would break C1. Missing or unexpected ref/checkpoint changes require recovery or explicit mapping acceptance; a `complete` marker alone cannot explain them.

For a first local realization, one common-directory interprocess lock plus durable rereads is a plausible smaller mechanism than a service or local generation protocol. It is not yet verified here. Hold ownership through the local dispatch-state transition and effect/receipt recording; if a request becomes unknown, its durable barrier survives release. A process that resumes later must reacquire ownership and reevaluate current state. Unique temporary paths may prevent file collisions but cannot prevent L28's serialized duplicate dispatch. None of these measures fences another clone or a delayed provider write. Cooperative exclusion of other clones remains an operational restriction whose usefulness must be evaluated.

**Apply uncertainty to responses and storage errors.** A received response, transport exception or local durability exception must be interpreted according to the result it actually establishes. The observed mutation 5xx responses do not establish rejection. Local TLS send completion does not establish server receipt. Positive attributable effects can support the corresponding bounded conclusion; absence, quiet reads or a new evidence label cannot turn an unknown earlier intent into a fresh safe retry. Any settlement of one effect must remain distinct from permission to overlap possible later effects.

**Clarify managed history ownership.** The bound artifact ID and expected identity are client evidence. They are not a server fence against authorized editors, nor is a marker unique by construction. Preserve marker-conflict handling, deliberate reattachment and the explicit policy that participants edit human prose while GHerrit edits its managed history artifact. Unexpected managed-artifact edits need conflict/recovery handling within that policy; a body section needs its own concurrent-edit protocol before replacing the selected default. Do not impose that new protocol merely because an actor has the technical permission to violate the cooperation policy.

**Replace superseded evidence without changing the ownership model.** Update queue configuration conclusions, confirmed dequeue behavior, genuine unacknowledged effects and natural 5xx outcomes; record tested CI alternatives and their prerequisites; distinguish automatic prefix from explicit website review/signing outcomes; retain actual distinct-account comment permissions and duplicate-marker evidence. Preserve the unexplained incompatible clock values and explicit uncalibrated-clock limits on derived timing. Keep exact source/case identities, prior contradictions and contaminated dispositions visible. No general provider or human result may be inferred.

The admission clarification may count as a semantic precision change if the coordinator judges the incumbent's wording insufficiently explicit about internal workers. It adds no new owner, identity kind, recovery phase or concurrency promise. Its implementation and verification burden must still be counted. Report updates alone are evidence-layer changes.

## Counterexamples to attractive simplifications

| Simplification under test | Concrete failure or burden that must be retained |
| --- | --- |
| Replace operation records with desired-state reconciliation and idempotent retries. | The 99-create 504 already created group 330. A relabeled identical create is the same unknown intent, not new authority. PR/comment creation can create duplicates; seeing a desired value does not identify its author in multiwriter scope. The API supplies no general idempotency key or future fence here. |
| Treat one checkout, a lock name or atomically written JSON as serialization. | In L28, both processes read `planned`; W1 applies and completes; W2 resumes cached state and applies again. Complete JSON and serialized provider recording still produce two effects. Ownership and state reread must cover the dispatch decision, with enforcement at the worker. |
| Repair completion by freezing the live continuation ref at its original result forever. | The author makes a valid later amend/rebase on that continuation. The original accepted result remains a valid integration baseline, while the current range legitimately differs. Validation must retain that distinction and accept the mapping through preparation, rather than making ordinary authoring look like corruption. |
| Use atomic Git pushes/leases as operation ownership. | L20 omits an equal head guard while creating an archive; L21 permits old→other→old ABA; an identical stale attempt may succeed as up-to-date. These primitives couple transmitted refs, not operation identity, metadata or delayed native effects. A generation protocol added to repair this would itself require authority and validation. |
| Reduce several revision roles to one current SHA or net tree. | The `x=0 → x=1 → x=2` import with pending `y=1` loses the correct integration baseline; cumulative equality can also hide B owning an upstream edit. L02/L03/L09 preserve change-local evidence, not just tip equality. Multi-commit success flattens to one commit and is no evidence for preserving all intermediate messages/identities. |

Configured REBASE/build-two did not deliver two actual builds. Separate group numbers can still share managed heads, trunks, public targets or checkpoints. The incumbent's effect-scope gate remains, with its availability cost. |
| Split every continuation onto fresh heads and declare it disjoint. | For landed old A with pending A′ and dependent surviving B′, new-ID F must precede canonical B. Rebuilding B or changing its base still overlaps B's possible native effects. Replacing B's PR would explicitly change C2's default and fragment discussion. Fresh refs help genuinely disjoint new work; they do not eliminate the F-before-B obligation. |
| Delegate publication/recovery to the released CLI or a service. | The inspected broad CLI path omits the coupled archive vector and can change more than membership. Either could earn its cost; neither is a demonstrated elimination of these duties. |

These are comparisons against obligations, not claims that every broader design must use the incumbent's mechanisms. A materially different candidate could meet them with a lower total burden; it must exhibit that difference and a useful repeated workflow.

## Whole-system burden and preserved workflow

No change retains substantial costs: single-publisher operations, exact local custody, four evidence roles/five revision roles, archive allocation, separate comment identity/update/recovery, whole-chain structural parking, range import, continuation selection, pinned CI/provider adapters and blocked overlap without a handoff. The current implementation is incomplete, so migration to this design is not zero-cost simply because the proposal is incumbent.

Surgical repair adds concrete boundary validation, interprocess ownership and adversarial recovery checks. It removes incorrect authority inferred from marker state, separate preflight or an HTTP error. That is an implementation cost and a reduction in unsafe admitted executions, not a demonstrated reduction in total code, latency or human effort. It avoids introducing a service, new archive format, replacement PR generations or a duplicate native topology model without discriminating evidence for their value.

| Contract area | Surgical candidate's supported coverage and remaining cost |
| --- | --- |
| C1, separate changes on one branch | Preserve one-commit linear authoring, explicit accepted mapping and optional hook invocation. Broader net-range fixtures support investigation, not silent acceptance of arbitrary multi-commit or nonlinear history. Preparation/continuation steps remain a user cost. |
| C2, identity/history | Preserve canonical PR bindings, immutable authored base/tip archives and explicit adoption. Validate actual destinations and namespaces. Prototype and structural evidence support components; production enforcement and reviewer interface remain incomplete. |
| C3, hosted ownership | GitHub owns membership/native landing, GHerrit publishes/reconciles, people own prose/readiness. Exclude the old cascade and enforce a cooperating publisher. Neither policy nor a local lock is a provider fence. |
| C4, partial/unknown effects | Preserve per-effect evidence, unknown barriers, actual scope and safe local capture. The five local recovery gaps must be repaired and rechecked before relying on the prototype. Power loss and future provider writes remain unestablished. |
| C5, useful repetition | Retain first publication, append, amendment, native prefix import, F-before-surviving-B insertion and fresh-group continuation as design obligations. Local/hosted demonstrations support the mechanisms; the overlapping-write handoff blocks the complete repeat-workflow claim. A read-only/local-import/limited-publication pilot must name its restricted boundary. |
| C6, burden | Count the retained and new work above for this candidate and alternatives. Shared provider limits do not favor this candidate, and a stronger alternative must not be penalized merely for exposing them. |
| C7, evidence | Keep documented promises, contradictory observations, local mechanisms and unavailable studies separate. Case dispositions include reused, negative, unavailable and replaced-premise cases; they are not 130 fresh successes. |
| C8, reversible adoption | Prefer new native chains; explicitly adopt existing bindings/history and exclude old effects. Transfer/rollback retains pending evidence and accepted checkpoints, and must pass the repaired admission boundary. Unknown native work prevents automatic fallback to the old cascade. |

The incumbent's scope restrictions also remain costs: reorder/insert/drop/fold use explicit whole-chain structural operations; shared-prefix forks wait for prefix landing or an explicit copy/new-review choice; unsupported nonlinear inputs stop before effects with originals retained; more than 100 members need deliberate staging or refusal without an atomic-landing claim. Completed and partial groups require actual lifecycle/residual-membership inspection. None of these policies becomes broader support merely because a net-range importer or one structural fixture succeeds.

The smallest defensible recommendation is therefore **retain the incumbent's direction, tighten dispatch-boundary obligations, update the evidence, and keep rollout restricted**. This does not discharge C5. A design comparison can recommend that qualified direction while stopping short of full-workflow or production acceptance. Root must decide whether additional supported handoff/recovery evidence or a genuinely lower-burden alternative is available; no architecture name, finite quiet window or unperformed human study can supply it.
