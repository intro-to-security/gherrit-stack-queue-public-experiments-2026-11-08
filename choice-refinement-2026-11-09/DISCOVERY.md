## Important discriminating observations

The reports retain exact conditions and source identities; inspect them before making a stronger inference.

1. The local recovery prototype accepted invalid completion, recreated missing operation evidence, accepted an operation identity mismatch, and duplicated dispatch from stale workers. A separate structural executor completed nine after-ack SIGKILL recoveries and refused two damaged/stale controls. These facts challenge enforcing boundaries; they do not identify one mandatory storage engine or prove unknown-write recovery.
2. Genuine no-HTTP-read requests produced effects. Direct 99-/100-member creates returned 504/502 while creating exact groups. The original blind retry violated the plan; original evidence and corrected guard remain. Group creation capacity 100 is separate from large-stack landing.
3. Git atomic pushes protect transmitted refs, not PR metadata, membership or future native writes. Equal refs can be omitted from effective guards, and value leases permit ABA. Canonical identity and incorporation cannot be inferred from a SHA or trailer alone.
4. Direct native prefix landing and queue admission are different paths. Terminal enqueued survives actual merges/ejections/dequeues. Queue SQUASH differed by tested build concurrency; REBASE's configured second build did not actually run. An explicit new-head admission worked in a disposable fixture; that does not choose an intent policy or establish a handoff.
5. Upper/middle GraphQL dequeue failed after confirmed admission. Actual signed-in upper/middle website confirmations separately returned visible Not Found, with ABC retained after each. A single lower dequeue removed dependents before timeout. No browser HTTP status/UUID or internal cause was captured. Failed pending removal did not cancel later admission.
6. Real push checks and a stacked target workflow with the required default-branch definition supplied positive first-CI routes. Producer identity, source/candidate SHA and actual gate mattered. Actual CodeQL and deployment controls do not support one universal check-precedence rule.
7. Three website fixtures retained/rebound existing reviews, while all four produced eight verified replacements. Matched automatic-prefix stale-review controls dismissed unchanged approvals. Records can stay APPROVED while a separate last-push policy blocks. These are bounded paths, not universal signing/retention guarantees.
8. Distinct-actor writes preserved separate comment/body payloads under programmed interleaving. A bot could edit user-authored comments; markers can be copied. The informed browser reached exact old patches and labeled comparisons with parent drift. Human discovery/comprehension remains unmeasured.
9. Repeated range import and the hosted F-before-surviving-B workflow retain local amendments and canonical B, but the incumbent leaves later overlapping republication gated. Any claimed replacement must explain the useful repeated trace, not merely rename the gate or refuse forever.
